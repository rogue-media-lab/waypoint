# RE7R01B 7-Speed AT — Drain & Fill Procedure

**Platforms:** 2011+ Infiniti QX56/QX80, Nissan Armada, Nissan Titan (Nissan Matic S fluid)

## Overview

The RE7R01B (JR711E) is a sealed 7-speed automatic — **no dipstick, no top-side fill port**. Both drain and fill happen from underneath the transmission pan via a standpipe/overflow design.

## Fluid Spec

- **Nissan Matic S ATF only.** Do NOT substitute — this transmission is fluid-sensitive.
- **Capacity:** ~4.0–4.5 quarts for a drain-and-fill (pan only, no torque converter drain).
- **Cost:** ~$10–14/qt. Dealer service with Matic S fluid runs $320–$350.

## Plug Layout (Under Pan)

| Plug | Location | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| Drain plug | Bottom of pan (larger hex) | Drains fluid from pan |
| Overflow/standpipe plug | Bottom of pan (smaller hex, inboard of drain) | Sets fluid level via internal standpipe; doubles as fill port |

## Procedure

1. **Warm transmission** to operating temp (brief drive).
2. **Remove fill/overflow plug first** (smaller hex) — ensures you can refill before committing to drain.
3. **Remove drain plug** — expect ~4 quarts. Catch and measure what comes out.
4. **Reinstall drain plug** with new crush washer (same copper washer as oil pan drain plug).
5. **Thread fill adapter** into the overflow plug bore. Nissan special tool J-49829, or aftermarket equivalent (OTC 7425, or similar Nissan transmission fill adapter).
6. **Pump in ~4 quarts** of Matic S using a fluid transfer pump. Hand pumps work but are slow; pneumatic dispenser preferred.
7. **Remove fill adapter** — let excess fluid drain until it slows to a trickle. This sets the correct level via the internal standpipe.
8. **Reinstall overflow plug** with new crush washer.
9. **Torque both plugs** to spec.

## Special Tools

- Nissan J-49829 Transmission Fill Pump (or aftermarket equivalent, e.g., OTC 7425 fill adapter + fluid pump)
- Fluid transfer pump capable of pushing ATF upward against gravity

## Key Pitfalls

- **Do not overfill.** The standpipe sets level automatically — let it overflow/drip to level.
- **New crush washers required** on both plugs. Same part as engine oil pan drain plug washer.
- **Temperature matters.** Factory procedure calls for fluid level check at a specific ATF temp range (requires Consult scan tool). For a simple drain-and-fill where you match the drained volume, this is less critical.
- **Matic S only.** BG fluid or universal ATF will cause shift quality issues and potential damage.
